This datapack contains custom crafting recipes, and some were created with Lazy Recipes Editor.
Recipes are organized by mod namespace to ensure compatibility.

This is a very important datapack! It is the sister datapack to S&S Behavior, 
and if you are playing Silk and Stardust, both are required for the modpack to work as intended!

If you decide to mess around in here, just know what you're doing.
